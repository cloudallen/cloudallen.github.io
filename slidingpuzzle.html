Slide the cars and trucks so the red car can drive out the exit.

Play any puzzle from the database

Paste a 36-character board string, read row by row from the top left: o is empty, x is a wall, A is the red car, and B–Z are the other vehicles.

Puzzles are taken from Michael Fogleman's Rush Hour puzzle database, which lists every "interesting" 6×6 starting position (2,577,412 puzzles, up to two walls) together with its optimal number of moves. The "Hardest" set contains the record puzzles from that database: 51 moves with no walls, and 60 moves with one wall. "The Classic" is this page's original level, which turns out to be that 51-move record puzzle.
